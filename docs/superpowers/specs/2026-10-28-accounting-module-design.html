How an event becomes an entry

Posting engine

The kernel owns the mechanism and knows no module by name. The accounting module plugs in as the ledger provider and is the only thing that writes its tables, through its own processes.

The rule template, as the admin sees it

Declarative JSON: roles, sides, paths into the event payload, dimensions for the mapping. No code runs.

RolLadoMontoDimensión
receivableDébito$.totals.total—
revenueCrédito$.lines[*].subtotalcategory:{{categoryId}}
vat_outCrédito$.taxes[*].amounttax:{{code}}

Dimensions let IVA 19 and IVA 5 land on different subcuentas and revenue follow the product category, with no account column on products or taxes.

Maestros

Cuenta account

  • code PUC, levels by length 1·2·4·6·8+
  • nature débito / crédito
  • allows_movement only subcuenta or auxiliar
  • requires_party, requires_cost_center, requires_open_auxiliary, requires_item, requires_business_unit
  • statement_section from the pack layout
  • access open / restricted, inherited by the subtree

Seguridad por cuenta account_permission

  • Owner 2026-10-28 · grants on a restricted account per role or user: can_view, can_post
  • User grant wins over role; nearest ancestor with access set decides
  • Enforced in the views: reports aggregate "Cuentas restringidas", records mask the lines, lookups omit them; engine exempt

Movimiento (consulta) ledger_line · v_ledger_lines

  • Owner 2026-10-29 · read-only query entity over a view joining line, entry, account, party and the four analytic dimensions
  • Every column filterable and sortable; debit, credit, net, signed_net aggregatable
  • Account security applied inside the view (can_view_account)
  • No table, no process of its own beyond ledger_line.list

Mapeo de cuentas account_role_mapping

  • role receivable, revenue, vat_out…
  • dimension tax:IVA_19 · category:uuid · payment_method:…
  • account_id must allow movement
  • Resolution: explicit → (role, dim) → (role) → failure

Fuente journal

  • code NC · VT · CP · RC · CE · AP · CI
  • kind general, sales, receipts, opening, closing…
  • next_number own gap-free consecutivo
  • manual_entries false for AP and CI

Centro de costo cost_center

  • code, name, parent_id tree
  • kind group / leaf, lines on leaves only
  • is_active; no default: a missing one is a visible failure

Auxiliar abierto open_auxiliary

  • Owner 2026-10-28 · analytic sub-ledger: projects, contracts, vehicles, employees
  • code, name, parent_id, kind group / leaf, is_active
  • Optional on any line; required per account flag

Ítem item

  • Owner 2026-10-28 · what was sold or bought, independent of the catalogue
  • Same shape as the cost center
  • Hidden until use_items is on in Configuración

Unidad de negocio business_unit

  • Owner 2026-10-28 · lines of business across cost centers
  • Same shape; the only one also on the comprobante header: lines inherit it and may override; resolved value stored on every line at post
  • Hidden until use_business_units is on

Daily life

Workflows

1

Puesta en marcha

Enable the module → Configurar contabilidad (TOTP) → review mapping and default rules → add auxiliares and cost centers → opening balances arrive as drafts in Apertura (phase 2b) and the accountant posts them under step-up.

2

Contabilización automática

Order confirmed → invoice issued → Ventas entry; COGS → Notas; receipt → Recibos de caja. The accountant filters Comprobantes by Hoy and jumps from Origen to the invoice. A failure shows on the badge and the home widget; fix the cause, Reintentar.

3

Nota de contabilidad manual

Nuevo → NC, date, detalle → lines with account and party lookups, live difference → attach the soporte → Contabilizar. Or from a template, or by asking the assistant "registra la provisión de arriendo de octubre por $ 2.500.000".

4

Reversión

Reversar on a posted entry: date and reason → a mirrored entry, both linked. Document voids reverse through their own event.

5

Cierre mensual

Checklist: borradores 0 · pendientes 0 · saldos de naturaleza contraria → TOTP → closed. Reabrir with TOTP, the whole month or one day; later balances follow automatically; the counter shows how often.

6

Cierre de año

12 periods closed → preview: classes 4–7 zeroed against 5905, then 5905 to 3605 (utilidad) or 3610 (pérdida) → TOTP → year closed, next year open. Moving the result to 3705 / 3710 after the asamblea is a templated manual entry.

7

Informes

Pick a report → parameters → grid → drill: trial balance row → auxiliar → entry → document. Export PDF, Excel, CSV; save the parameter set as a view.

8

Cambiar una regla

Edit lines → Probar con un evento real → preview → Guardar (TOTP). Or tell the assistant "las ventas de Bebidas van a la 413520": it proposes the mapping as a card and asks for the code.

9

¿Qué pasó con la factura FE-1234?

From the invoice's Contabilidad tab, or by asking: the assistant reads the entries and the audit chain and explains the order → invoice → entry → receipt sequence.

10

Exógena del año y cartera vencida

Conceptos exógena all green → Informes → Información exógena → formato, año → errors panel empty → Exportar XML for the prevalidador. Cartera por edades by customer, click a row for its open invoices; "¿quién me debe más de 90 días?" to the assistant.

11

Cuatro ojos y acumulaciones

Andrés drafts the provisión and sends it to approval; Laura opens Por aprobar, reviews and posts it (she cannot post her own). An accrual carries Reversar el 01/11: posting it leaves the mirrored draft in November. Cerrar octubre runs the control tie-outs and the suspense check and records each as a sign-off row.

12

Seguridad por cuenta

Laura restricts 5105 Gastos de personal to contador and gerencia (Seguridad tab, TOTP). Andrés, auxiliar contable, then sees one line "Cuentas restringidas" in the Balance de prueba, masked lines on the payroll comprobante, no 5105 in his lookups, and totals that still equal Laura's.

13

Consultar movimientos

Movimientos → Cuenta 5105, Centro de costo en (Ventas Bogotá, Ventas Medellín), Tercero contiene "Nómina" → sort Fecha, then Débito ↓ (shift-click) → Agrupar por Cuenta, Centro de costo → two levels of subtotals with Σ débito, Σ crédito, Σ neto and count → Guardar vista "Gastos de personal por sede" → Excel keeps the groups. Marcela gets zero lines and the omitted-lines note.
