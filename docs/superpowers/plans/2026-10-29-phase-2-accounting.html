Scope

Phase 2 is three plans

Each produces working software on its own. 2A is the only one written; 2B and 2C are named so 2A builds what they need without building them.

Until 2B exists, the §5.4 default rule templates ship as accounting fixtures (packages/modules/accounting/src/rules/defaults/*.json) and the engine is tested with synthetic outbox events. 2B moves each template into its own manifest unchanged.

Review focus

Five things that would bite an accountant

The failure modes the spec implies but no ordinary test exercises. Each one has a named test in the task that owns the code; a reviewer checks these first.

1 · Redelivery and retry post once

The same outbox row dispatched twice, a retry of a resolved failure, and two rules on one event leave exactly one entry per (event, rule).

T20 redelivered event answers the first entryT20 two rules, two entries

2 · Closed periods never move an entry

A document dated in a closed period fails with closed_period from engine, UI, assistant, import and template; a reopen by day admits only that day; a credit note posts in its own month.

T19 post refuses closed periodT21 reopened_for admits only that dayT20 reverseOf posts in the note's period

3 · Restricted accounts leak nothing and lose no money

Statements show one aggregated "Cuentas restringidas" line with the admin's totals; line reports, Movimientos, exports and data.query return no line and no amount; a forbidden account is refused by name.

T17 v_ledger_lines hides restricted linesT18 account_forbiddenT22 totals equalT25 every group level

4 · Four-eyes and auto-reversal are exact

The creator cannot post their own manual entry while another user can; engine postings ignore it; a reverse_on entry leaves exactly one mirrored draft even when the post is retried.

T19 same_user_approvalT19 engine ignores four-eyesT19 one mirrored draft, idempotent

5 · Close is a gate, balances are computed

Close refuses on drafts, pending approvals, pending failures, undispatched events, control differences, suspense ≠ 0 or a contra-nature balance, records every check with figures and closes in order; year close zeroes 4–7 through 5905 into 3605 / 3610; a post into a reopened period moves later openings with no recalculation.

T21 close refuses and records checksT21 year close lands on 3605 or 3610T21 reopened post moves later opening balances
Pinned by the spec

Global constraints every task inherits

Data and boundaries

  • Rule 7 columns on every table: uuid v7 id, company_id, seq, timestamps, created_by / updated_by, extra, extra_free; money numeric(18,4); RLS and row audit through the kernel helpers.
  • Published views only across modules: v_account_balances, v_entries_for_source, v_tax_lines, v_open_items, v_ledger_lines; no cross-schema foreign key.
  • Fiscal data only in the pack: PUC, role codes, validation, calendar, layouts, journals, tax codes, exógena. A PUC code in kernel or module source fails check:rules.

Processes and security

  • Engine writes only through entry.post_from_event and posting_failure.record, both origins: ["event"], never step-up.
  • Step-up on chart seed, role and concept mappings, account permissions, period and year close / reopen, posting rules, opening post, settings. Admin may raise post, reverse, account update.
  • Enums exact: status draft · pending · posted · reversed · voided; origin manual · auto · import · template; the twelve failure codes; the ten close checks; the role catalogue plus suspense.

Defaults

  • Journals NC VT CP RC CE AP CI; manual journal NC; rounding tolerance 1,0000 COP; NIIF group 2; four-eyes off; the three optional dimensions off.
  • Aging buckets por vencer · 1–30 · 31–60 · 61–90 · > 90; round-amount flag at $ 1.000.000; groupBy ≤ 3, sort ≤ 5; Movimientos opens on the open period, posted, by date, number, line.

Copy, menu, method

  • Spanish es-419 through packages/i18n with an en mirror; the journal master is Fuente / Fuentes; Libro diario keeps its name; money $ 1.234.567,00.
  • Six bar items: Comprobantes · Movimientos · Maestros ▾ · Períodos · Informes · Reglas contables.
  • Tests first; migrations forward-only NNNN_accounting_<topic>.sql; one commit per task, none without the owner's ask; no feature without its guide.
